About VYBE OS & Productivity Studio

Empowering creators, students, and professionals with privacy-first browser utilities.

Image Studio Engine

VYBE Image Studio is a 100% client-side web application designed to compress, resize, and convert image files (JPG, PNG, WebP) directly inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas APIs. No media files are ever uploaded or saved on external servers, ensuring complete user privacy and instantaneous processing speeds.

Focus & Audio Synthesizer

The integrated Focus Room combines proven Pomodoro time management techniques with synthesized ambient noise generators (Rain & 432Hz Drone) created dynamically via the browser Web Audio API. This helps maintain deep concentration during long study or coding sessions without external media downloads.

Data Security & Storage

VYBE OS operates on a zero-tracking architecture. Your notes, custom app shortcuts, priority tasks, and personal preferences remain securely stored within your own browser's LocalStorage. You retain total control over your data with one-click data purge options.
