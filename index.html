About VYBE

VERSION 3.0

A personal productivity dashboard designed to help you capture ideas, manage tasks and protect your focus. Your workspace data stays in this browser's local storage unless you export it.

There is no account system or cloud sync in this single-file version. Clearing browser site data may erase locally saved information, so export backups regularly.
